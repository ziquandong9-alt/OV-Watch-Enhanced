# AI-assisted development disclosure

AI tools participated materially in this project's implementation, refactoring, commenting and documentation.

The maintainer's contribution and responsibility include:

- defining requirements and deciding product behavior;
- building and wiring the physical hardware;
- reproducing visual, timing, power and interaction problems on the watch;
- measuring FPS and comparing alternative implementations;
- selecting trade-offs under SPI, Flash, RAM, EEPROM and power constraints;
- compiling, flashing, testing and accepting or rejecting generated changes;
- reviewing the resulting code and being responsible for published behavior.

The repository does not claim that every line was manually typed without assistance. Instead, it aims to make the engineering decisions, measurements, limitations and provenance inspectable. Bugs and design decisions remain the maintainer's responsibility.
